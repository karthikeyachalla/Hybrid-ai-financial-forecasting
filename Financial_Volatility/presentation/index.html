Why Volatility Forecasting Fails

Standard models assume CONSTANT VARIANCE — markets don't. GARCH mathematically fails during black-swan events.

Neural Limitations

LSTM alone misses rigid mathematical structure. Generates a strict 72.1% hit rate ceiling without baseline flooring.

How the Hybrid Model Works

EGARCH Module

  • Models conditional variance σ²t utilizing arch_model
  • Captures leverage effect: negative news directly translates to larger volatility spikes
  • Provides mathematical "floor" for the neural network preventing hallucinations

LSTM Module

  • PyTorch dual-LSTM blocks, utilizing 128 hidden channels
  • Adam optimized, configured with a 10-step chronological lookback
  • Captures intense, non-linear human sentiment shifts embedded in price patterns

Ensemble Formula: f(EGARCH(R_t), LSTM(R_t))

— Synthesized by inverse-MSE precision weighting —
